Privacy notice - Swiss PecuniaX (personal)

Last updated: 22 November 2026

What this application is

Swiss PecuniaX (personal) is a personal finance application that runs on one computer and is used by one person: its owner, who is also the person whose bank accounts are read. It is not offered to the public, has no other users and has no server of its own.

Who is responsible

The owner of the application, Massimiliano Marchesi, contact: info@dopaistudiox.com.

What data is used

With the explicit consent of the account holder, given at the bank, the application reads the account information of the holder's own accounts through the Enable Banking open banking service: account identifiers, balances and transactions. It does not initiate payments.

Where the data goes

  • The data is stored only on the owner's computer, in an encrypted database. It is not uploaded to any server of the application.
  • The data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising, profiling or analytics.
  • Requests to the bank pass through Enable Banking, which processes them as a service provider to give access to the bank.

How long

Until the owner deletes it. The owner can delete the data at any time in the application, and withdraw the consent given to the bank at any time, at the bank or by letting it expire.

Rights

The account holder can ask for access, correction or deletion of the data, and withdraw consent, by writing to the contact above. Complaints can be addressed to the data protection authority of the holder's country.
